## 4. Services

EIA Therapeutic Haven provides structured, confidential, online support sessions. Sessions are organized around three core client needs:

1. **Emotional support / counseling** — processing what a client is experiencing in a structured, safe way.
2. **Stress or anxiety management** — addressing pressure that builds up silently over time.
3. **Life clarity or direction** — helping clients who feel stuck or uncertain about their next steps.

**What clients receive:**
- Confidential support
- Emotional clarity sessions
- A structured guidance process
- Online support available globally

**Scope boundary:** Sessions are structured for emotional support, clarity, and wellness guidance. This is **not emergency psychiatric care** — in urgent cases, an appropriate referral is recommended.

## 6. Client Journey

The full pathway is designed so the system attracts and filters clients even when the practitioner isn't actively chatting.

```
Ads bring people in
        ↓
WhatsApp automation filters them
        ↓
Conversation qualifies them
        ↓
Booking closes them
        ↓
Payment secures the session
        ↓
Intake form prepares the session
        ↓
Session delivers value
        ↓
Referrals expand the client base
```

### Step-by-step

1. **Discovery** — Prospect sees a paid ad or organic content and messages "HELP" on WhatsApp.
2. **Auto-greeting & menu** — Automated welcome message asks whether they need (1) emotional support, (2) stress/anxiety management, or (3) life clarity.
3. **Tailored auto-response** — A specific, empathetic follow-up is sent based on the 1 / 2 / 3 reply, ending with a question that invites the client to open up.
4. **Qualification** — After the client responds, they're told a structured emotional clarity session is a good starting point.
5. **Booking** — Available session slots offered (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday).
6. **Payment** — Payment is required *before* booking confirmation, ensuring proper scheduling and preparation.
7. **Post-payment intake** — Client receives the intake form link to complete before the session.
8. **Session delivery** — The practitioner reviews intake data and delivers a prepared, structured session.

## 8. Client Intake Form

A structured, clinical-tone intake questionnaire designed to filter serious clients, understand emotional needs quickly, and prepare for sessions properly. Intended to run via Google Forms (recommended), a WhatsApp link form, or a website embed.

**Section 1 — Personal Information:** Full name; age; gender (optional); location (city & country); phone/WhatsApp; email; occupation.

**Section 2 — Reason for Seeking Support:** What brings you here now? When did the issue begin? Is it ongoing or recently triggered?

**Section 3 — Emotional Experience:** Tick all that apply — stress, anxiety, emotional overwhelm, sadness/low mood, confusion/lack of clarity, anger/irritability, sleep difficulties, relationship struggles, life direction uncertainty. Plus a 1–10 emotional distress rating.

**Section 4 — Impact on Daily Life:** How is this affecting daily functioning (work, relationships, sleep, motivation, decision-making)? Which part of life feels most affected?

**Section 5 — Previous Support:** Have you spoken to a counselor, therapist, or pastor before? (Yes/No, and experience). Currently on any mental/emotional health medication? (Yes/No, specify).

**Section 6 — Coping & Support System:** Current coping methods. Strength of support system (Strong / Moderate / Weak / None).

**Section 7 — Goals for Counseling:** Desired outcomes — emotional stability, clarity and direction, stress reduction, better decision-making, improved relationships, other. Plus: what would "feeling better" look like?

**Section 8 — Optional Faith Integration:** Would you like faith-based guidance included when appropriate? (Yes / No / Maybe).

**Section 9 — Confidentiality & Consent:** Acknowledges strict confidentiality, that sessions are for emotional support/clarity/wellness guidance, and that this is not emergency psychiatric care (referral recommended in urgent cases). Requires agreement (Yes/No).

**Final Section:** How did you hear about us? (Social media, referral, WhatsApp, Google, other). Plus space for any additional information for the practitioner.
